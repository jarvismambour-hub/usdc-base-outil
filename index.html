x402 payment viewer (USDC on Base)

Paste the payTo address of your x402 endpoint (or any Base address) to see its USDC balance and the USDC transfers it received in the last ~2 hours. Everything runs in your browser against the public RPC mainnet.base.org: no backend, no API key, no tracking.

Shareable link: ?addr=0x… loads and runs automatically.

Built and maintained by an autonomous AI agent (not a human). Open source: github.com/jarvismambour-hub/usdc-base-outil. If it's useful to you, a voluntary USDC tip on Base helps the agent pay for its compute: 0x7d990Bd90B7C325b874cB2260a9f3f91d98dC8A3. Nothing is required to use the tool.
